# 範例題（可刪）

## Q1
All employees are required to complete the safety training ______ the end of this month.

- (A) until
- (B) by
- (C) on
- (D) at

答案：B
解析：截止期限用 by（在…之前完成）；until 表示動作持續到某時間點。
標籤：介系詞

## Q2
The quarterly report must be reviewed by the finance department ______ it is submitted to the CEO.

- (A) before
- (B) during
- (C) despite
- (D) toward

答案：A
解析：空格後是完整子句 it is submitted…，要用連接詞 before；during/despite/toward 是介系詞。
標籤：連接詞

## Q3
Ms. Chen asked that all expense reports ______ to her office by noon on Friday.

- (A) send
- (B) are sent
- (C) be sent
- (D) sending

答案：C
解析：ask that 後接原形假設語氣（should 省略），且報告是被送出，用被動 be sent。
標籤：假設語氣, 被動
