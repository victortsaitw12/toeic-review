# Carol Bai 多益文法 Part 5

## Day1-Q2
The deadline for submitting the project analysis is next Friday. Please make sure to meet the ______.

- (A) datable
- (B) dating
- (C) deadline
- (D) deadliness

答案：C
解析：meet the deadline「趕上截止期限」，空格需名詞 deadline。
標籤：詞性, 名詞

## Day2-Q1
If you want to ______ your claim, you need to provide more evidence.

- (A) substantiate
- (B) substantial
- (C) substance
- (D) substantially

答案：A
解析：受詞 your claim 前需動詞，substantiate「證實、證明」；substantial 形容詞、substance 名詞。
標籤：詞性, 動詞

## Day3-Q2
Our sales team needs to be ______ in their approach to customers to increase satisfaction.

- (A) proactive
- (B) proactivity
- (C) proactiveness
- (D) proactively

答案：A
解析：be 動詞後接形容詞當補語，proactive「積極主動的」。
標籤：詞性, 形容詞

## Day4-Q2
Our company holds meetings ______ to ensure that everyone is well informed.

- (A) regularity
- (B) regularly
- (C) regular
- (D) regulate

答案：B
解析：修飾動詞 holds 用副詞 regularly「定期地」。
標籤：詞性, 副詞

## Day13-Q13
The company provided ______ with a comprehensive training program to enhance their skills.

- (A) they
- (B) their
- (C) them
- (D) themselves

答案：C
解析：provide sb with sth，動詞的受詞用受格 them。
標籤：代名詞, 受格

## Day15-Q23
Our company's customer satisfaction ratings are higher than ______ of our main rivals.

- (A) which
- (B) whose
- (C) what
- (D) those

答案：D
解析：代替前面複數名詞 ratings，用 those（than those of…）。
標籤：代名詞, 指示代名詞

## Day21-Q55
The marketing team needs to finalize the campaign strategy ______ the budget meeting.

- (A) unlike
- (B) without
- (C) before
- (D) within

答案：C
解析：在預算會議「之前」敲定策略，用 before。
標籤：介系詞, before

## Day26-Q84
Please be advised that the office will be closed ______ the weekend for renovations.

- (A) under
- (B) alongside
- (C) about
- (D) during

答案：D
解析：during + 期間名詞 the weekend，表「在…期間」。
標籤：介系詞, during

## Day31-Q13
Please submit your expense reports ______ attending the company training seminar.

- (A) although
- (B) underneath
- (C) after
- (D) throughout

答案：C
解析：after + V-ing，表「在…之後」after attending。
標籤：介系詞, after

## Day35-Q32
The seminar will be targeted ______ improving leadership skills for middle and upper management.

- (A) for
- (B) in
- (C) at
- (D) with

答案：C
解析：be targeted at「以…為目標、針對」。
標籤：介系詞, at

## Day39-Q8
______ the shipment was delayed, the team completed the project on time.

- (A) Even though
- (B) Instead of
- (C) In addition to
- (D) On top of

答案：A
解析：後接完整子句表讓步「儘管」，用連接詞 Even though；其餘後接名詞。
標籤：連接詞, even though

## Day43-Q26
The company's new policy allows employees to work from home ______ come to the office.

- (A) and
- (B) or
- (C) with
- (D) since

答案：B
解析：在家或到辦公室，二擇一用對等連接詞 or。
標籤：連接詞, or

## Day45-Q36
ABC Consulting Firm provides a 5 percent discount ______ clients complete their payment within 15 days.

- (A) also
- (B) but
- (C) if
- (D) yet

答案：C
解析：條件句「如果…就給折扣」，用連接詞 if。
標籤：連接詞, if

## Day46-Q43
______ the necessary permits are obtained, the construction project cannot commence.

- (A) Mostly
- (B) At least
- (C) No more
- (D) Unless

答案：D
解析：unless「除非」引導條件，除非取得許可否則不能動工。
標籤：連接詞, unless

## Extra-Q1
The marketing team ______ a new strategy to increase brand awareness last quarter.

- (A) develop
- (B) developed
- (C) was developing
- (D) develops

答案：B
解析：last quarter 為明確過去時間，用過去簡單式 developed。
標籤：時態, 過去式

## Extra-Q2
The annual report ______ by the accounting department before the board meeting.

- (A) prepare
- (B) prepared
- (C) was prepared
- (D) prepares

答案：C
解析：報告是「被」準備的，用被動語態；配合過去時間用 was prepared。
標籤：語態, 被動
