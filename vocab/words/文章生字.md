# 閱讀文章生字（隨讀隨加）

讀英文文章時遇到的生字，連同同字根／近義字一起擴充。新字直接往表格下面加一列即可。

| 單字 | 詞性 | 中文釋義 | 級距 | 英文例句 | 例句翻譯 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| solidarity | n. | 團結；同心協力；（對某方的）聲援 | 4 | Workers at other plants walked out in a show of solidarity with the strikers. | 其他廠的工人也停工，以示聲援罷工者。 |
| in solidarity with | phr. | 聲援…；與…站在一起 | 4 | Staff wore black in solidarity with their laid-off colleagues. | 員工穿黑衣聲援被裁的同事。 |
| solidify | v. | 鞏固；使更穩固；凝固 | 4 | The joint project solidified the partnership between the two firms. | 這個合作案鞏固了兩家公司的夥伴關係。 |
| cohesion | n. | 凝聚力；團結一致 | 4 | Regular team lunches help build cohesion among departments. | 定期的團隊聚餐有助於凝聚各部門。 |
| cohesive | adj. | 有凝聚力的；結合緊密的 | 4 | The new manager turned a group of individuals into a cohesive team. | 新主管把一群個人變成一支有凝聚力的團隊。 |
| unity | n. | 團結；一致 | 3 | The mayor called for unity after the divisive election. | 市長在分裂的選舉後呼籲團結。 |
| unite | v. | 團結；聯合起來 | 2 | The crisis united residents who had never spoken before. | 這場危機讓從沒交談過的居民團結起來。 |
| collective | adj. | 集體的；共同的 | 3 | The union negotiates wages through collective bargaining. | 工會透過集體協商談判薪資。 |
| coalition | n. | 聯盟；（政黨）聯合陣線 | 4 | A coalition of local groups petitioned the city council. | 由地方團體組成的聯盟向市議會請願。 |
| alliance | n. | 聯盟；結盟關係 | 3 | The airline formed an alliance with three regional carriers. | 這家航空公司與三家區域航空結盟。 |
| rally | v. | 集結；號召支持；振作 | 4 | Volunteers rallied behind the campaign within days. | 志工在幾天內就集結起來支持這場活動。 |
| camaraderie | n. | 同伴情誼；袍澤之情 | 4 | Years of night shifts created a strong sense of camaraderie. | 多年的夜班培養出濃厚的同伴情誼。 |
| vigil | n. | 守夜；（悼念或祈禱的）守夜集會；徹夜守候 | 4 | Hundreds gathered outside the hospital for a candlelight vigil. | 數百人聚集在醫院外舉行燭光守夜。 |
| hold a vigil | phr. | 舉行守夜（悼念）活動；守候 | 4 | Neighbors held a vigil for the victims of the fire. | 鄰居們為火災罹難者舉行守夜悼念。 |
| vigilant | adj. | 警覺的；提高戒備的 | 4 | Staff were told to remain vigilant against phishing emails. | 員工被要求對釣魚郵件保持警覺。 |
| vigilance | n. | 警戒；戒備（之心） | 4 | Constant vigilance is required at the security checkpoint. | 安檢站需要持續的戒備。 |
| vigilante | n. | 私自執法者；自組治安隊的人 | 4 | Frustrated residents formed vigilante patrols after the burglaries. | 居民在竊案後自組治安巡邏隊。 |
| surveillance | n. | 監視；監控 | 4 | The building is under 24-hour video surveillance. | 這棟大樓有二十四小時錄影監控。 |
| mourn | v. | 哀悼；為…悲痛 | 3 | The town mourned the loss of its longtime doctor. | 全鎮哀悼這位老醫師的辭世。 |
| mourner | n. | 哀悼者；送葬者 | 4 | Mourners lined the street as the procession passed. | 送葬的人沿街站立，目送隊伍通過。 |
| tribute | n. | 致敬；悼念的表示 | 4 | Fans left flowers outside the theater as a tribute. | 粉絲在劇院外獻花致敬。 |
| condolences | n. | 弔唁；慰問之意 | 4 | The mayor offered her condolences to the families. | 市長向罹難者家屬致哀。 |
| bereaved | adj. | 喪親的；失去親人的 | 4 | Counselors were on hand to support bereaved relatives. | 現場有諮商師協助喪親的家屬。 |
